Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VoiceCalc AI's features, privacy, and architecture.

VoiceCalc AI uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (Android or iPhone) together with our own on-device math parser. Download your language's offline speech pack once (in the app: Settings → Offline Speech) and voice works fully offline, even in flight mode. Without that pack, your phone's speech service (Google or Apple) may process the audio. The calculation itself always runs on your phone, and the app never sends anything over the internet.
Simply tap the PiP button in the app's top bar or enable it in Settings. Android will ask for "Display Over Other Apps" permission once. Afterwards, a compact floating calculator will hover over WhatsApp, Amazon, or Excel. You can drag it anywhere, collapse it to a bubble, or tap to calculate while keeping your active app visible.
Standard calculators often trigger a reset error if you accidentally tap an operator like '+' or '×' right before pressing '='. VoiceCalc AI features our proprietary Pending Operator Safety engine: it safely computes the expression up to the last valid number or notifies you that the operator is still pending, ensuring your calculations are never accidentally lost.
