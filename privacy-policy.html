Privacy Policy for VoiceCalc AI

Effective and last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Summary

VoiceCalc AI does not collect any personal data. We, the developer, do not receive, store, sell or share your information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no AI or cloud service. Your calculations, history and settings stay on your device.
  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or AI/cloud SDKs inside the app.
  • The app does not send your data over the internet. Android release versions do not even include the Internet permission.
  • Voice is turned into text by your phone's own speech service. See section 5 for exactly how.

This policy applies to the VoiceCalc AI: Smart Calculator app for Android (Google Play, app ID com.voicecalcai.app) and iOS (App Store, bundle ID app.voicecalcai). “We”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of VoiceCalc AI. Contact details are in section 17.

4. Information stored only on your device

To work as a calculator, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Calculation history: each expression, its result, the words you spoke or typed, whether it came from voice or the keypad, and the time. Only the 200 most recent entries are kept.
  • Settings: theme, accent colour, sounds, haptics, language, speech rate and voice.
  • Exchange rates you type in for offline currency conversion.
  • The last result shown on the Android home-screen widget.

This data lives in the app's sandbox, which other apps cannot read. It never reaches us, and we cannot access it.

How long it is kept: until you delete it. You can delete one entry or tap Clear All in History. You can also clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Device backups: if you have turned on Android device backup (Google) or iCloud Backup, your phone may include this app data in your own backup. Google or Apple manage that backup under your account, and we have no access to it. You can turn backups off in your device settings.

5. Microphone, speech recognition and spoken answers

  • The microphone is used only after you turn voice on: with the mic button, the widget's voice button or the Quick Settings tile. Your phone shows its usual microphone-in-use indicator. Voice switches off automatically after about 15 seconds of silence, or when you tap the mic again.
  • Speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service. On Android this is the system speech service, usually Speech Services by Google. On iPhone it is Apple's Speech framework.
  • On-device when available: on Android 13 or later with the offline language pack installed (in the app: Settings → Offline Speech), recognition happens entirely on your phone. Older Android versions use the offline voice-typing pack if you have installed it. On iPhone, iOS uses on-device recognition when it supports your language.
  • If on-device recognition is not available, your phone's speech provider may process the audio on its own servers. That processing is covered by the provider's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy or Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive the audio or the text.
  • The app only receives the resulting text. It works out the calculation on your phone and never records or stores audio.
  • Spoken answers use your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. You can turn them off in Settings.

7. Clipboard, sharing, widget and floating calculator

  • Copy: the app writes a result to your clipboard only when you tap Copy or say “copy”. It never reads your clipboard.
  • Share: opens your phone's share sheet with the calculation text. You choose where it goes, and we don't see it.
  • Widget and Quick Settings tile (Android): show your last result and open the app. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Floating calculator (Android): draws its own small window. It cannot see, read or record what other apps show.

8. Internet and network use

  • The app does not need the internet. It has no servers, accounts or cloud processing.
  • Currency conversion uses only the exchange rates you type in. The app never downloads rates.
  • Android release versions do not include the Internet permission, so Android itself blocks the app from using the network.
  • The iPhone app makes no network requests of its own. System services you choose to use, such as Apple speech recognition, work as described in section 5.
  • App updates are delivered by Google Play or the App Store.

9. Third parties

  • The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting or AI.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app. If you have allowed it in your device settings (for example “Share with App Developers” on iPhone or “Usage & diagnostics” on Android), they may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These do not identify you, and we use them only to fix bugs and improve the app.
  • Speech recognition providers are described in section 5.
  • We do not sell or share personal information (including as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act), and we do not use any data for targeted advertising.

10. Children's privacy

VoiceCalc AI is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU or 18 under India's DPDP Act). It has no ads, no chat, no sign-in and no links to buy anything.

11. Your rights and choices

Privacy laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over your personal data. These include the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. Everything is on your device and under your control:

  • Delete history entries, or tap Clear All.
  • Turn off voice, spoken answers or haptics in Settings.
  • Revoke the microphone, speech or overlay permission in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its data.

If you have any privacy question or request, email us at ourcanvasapp@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

12. Data deletion

There is no account and no server-side data, so there is nothing for you to ask us to delete. To remove all app data from your device, tap Clear All in History and reset Settings, clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → VoiceCalc AI → Storage, or uninstall the app.

13. Security

Your data is kept in the operating system's private app sandbox and is protected by your phone's lock screen and built-in encryption. The app never transmits your data, so there is no server or transfer that could leak. Please keep your device and its backups secure.

14. International transfers

We do not transfer any personal data, because we do not receive any. If your phone's speech provider processes audio on its servers (section 5), that provider may do so in other countries, under its own privacy policy.

15. This website

The website voicecalcai.pages.dev is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site (Cloudflare Privacy Policy). The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers (Google Privacy Policy).

The live demo on the website runs the VoiceCalc AI calculation engine inside your browser. What you type is calculated on the page and is not sent to us or stored. If you tap the demo's microphone, your browser's own speech recognition turns your speech into text: in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge the audio is sent to Google or Microsoft for recognition, and in Safari to Apple, under their privacy policies (Google, Microsoft, Apple). Your browser asks for microphone permission first, and the demo works fully by typing if you prefer. The promotional videos are served from the website itself.

The website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. Its feedback form sends nothing by itself: it only opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing reaches us unless you press send there. To reach us, please email ourcanvasapp@gmail.com.
