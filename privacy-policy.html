Privacy Policy for VoiceCalc AI

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. Overview & Core Privacy Promise

VoiceCalc AI is engineered from the ground up with a strict privacy-by-design architecture. We believe that your daily numbers, personal finances, budget calculations, and spoken voice commands belong solely to you.

Zero Data Collection Guarantee: VoiceCalc AI does not collect, log, track, sell, or transmit any user data, calculation records, or audio recordings to external cloud servers. All computations run 100% locally on your device.

2. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

To provide hands-free voice calculation and multi-tasking overlay features, VoiceCalc AI requests the following explicit Android device permissions:

  • Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`): Used exclusively when you activate voice calculation to convert spoken words into mathematical operations in real time.
    How it is handled: Audio is processed in memory on your device using Android's system speech recognizer. We never save audio files to permanent storage, and we never upload your voice to any remote server or third-party cloud.
  • Display Over Other Apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`): Used solely to enable the optional Floating Overlay Mode, allowing a compact calculator bubble to float over applications like WhatsApp, Amazon, or Excel.
    How it is handled: This permission is used solely to render the calculator window on your screen. The app does not read, capture, screenshot, or inspect any content from the underlying applications.
  • Vibration (`VIBRATE`): Used to deliver crisp, hardware-accelerated tactile haptic feedback on keypad button presses.

3. Calculation Data & Local Storage

All calculations, variables, formulas, and history entries are saved strictly within your device's private sandboxed application storage (`AsyncStorage`).

  • No user account or registration is required to use the app.
  • Your calculation history is never synced to the cloud.
  • You can permanently wipe your calculation history at any time with a single tap using the "Clear All History" button in the app.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

VoiceCalc AI contains NO third-party analytics SDKs, NO advertising trackers, NO behavioral telemetry trackers, and NO data brokers. We do not track your location, your device identifiers, or your usage habits.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

VoiceCalc AI is completely safe for all audiences and does not collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age (or any age).

6. Data Security

Because all processing occurs strictly within the local Android OS sandbox without outbound network communication for calculations, your financial and computational data is naturally protected against interception, man-in-the-middle attacks, and remote server leaks.
